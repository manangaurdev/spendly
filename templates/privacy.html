Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or bank credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to generate summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database and retained for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages are loaded. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.
